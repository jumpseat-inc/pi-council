## Intent

The wiki's Ingest operation is human-steered by design: `vault/CLAUDE.md`
step 2 says "Discuss key takeaways with me BEFORE writing — what's new,
surprising, what it connects to", `council/procedures/wiki-ingest.md` repeats
that instruction, and the `wiki-ingest` eval fixture encodes the turn as a
policy gate (`humanStep: 1`, "the fixture plays the human discussion turn").
That is the right contract for a human-curated source under `vault/raw/`: the
human's steer is what makes the ingest say something the source alone does
not. It is the wrong contract for a council run's own record, whose takeaways
are derivable from the run rather than from anyone's memory, and which an
unattended run does not ingest at all today.

This card builds the second ingest class. A run-ledger ingest reads the run's
own durable record, writes the source summary and the affected entity and
concept pages, updates `vault/wiki/index.md`, and appends to
`vault/wiki/log.md` — the same Ingest bookkeeping the schema already defines
— with no human turn. Where the human steer would have been, the ingest
escalates: a judgment call it cannot derive from the record (a claim that
contradicts an existing wiki page, a page-name or scope decision) becomes a
question for the ruling seat that owns the wiki as grounding, and at
portfolio scope for the portfolio-level ruling seat. The card states which
of the two the escalation reaches and what the caller does while the
question is open.

Two constraints bound the design. First, the human-curated path keeps its
steer: this card adds a variant; it does not delete the discuss step from the
general operation or from the `wiki-ingest` fixture. Second, the run
substrate is ephemeral — `.pi/council/runs/` is self-gitignored and pruned to
the last 15 runs — while `vault/raw/` is immutable and git-tracked, so the
run's ledger artifact must exist under `vault/raw/` before the run directory
can be pruned.

The write owner is the run-ledger ingest operation itself, executed by the
completing agent at each trigger site — the attended parent session at step
14, `council-runner` inside a card container, and the orchestrator at
`/features-deliver` Phase 3. No seat is granted a `vault/wiki/` write and no
seat charter changes: the operation's own steps are the only `vault/` write
path, and the triggering step still forbids hand-editing `vault/`. Card-level
ingest judgment escalates to `product-owner`; anything that reaches past one
card escalates to `steward`.

Surface: the ingest's output is read copy — the source page's frontmatter and
`summary:` line, the escalation question, and the completion line a person
reads afterwards. The page conventions come from `vault/CLAUDE.md`; the card
must not invent a second set.

## Acceptance

- `vault/CLAUDE.md` documents the run-ledger ingest as an operation distinct
  from the human-curated Ingest: the same bookkeeping (source page, affected
  entity and concept pages, `vault/wiki/index.md`, `vault/wiki/log.md`), and
  no "discuss with me first" turn.
- The human-curated path is unchanged: `council/procedures/wiki-ingest.md`
  still requires the discussion turn, and `council/fixtures/wiki-ingest/`
  still carries its `ingest-discussion` human gate with `humanStep: 1`.
- A judgment call the ingest cannot derive from the run record escalates to
  `product-owner` (card level) or `steward` (portfolio level); the write owner
  is the ingest operation executed by the completing agent at the attended
  parent session, the `council-runner` container, or the `/features-deliver`
  orchestrator; the ingest never hand-edits `vault/` outside the operation's
  own steps.
- A run-ledger source under `vault/raw/` maps to exactly one
  `vault/wiki/sources/` page; re-ingesting the same ledger updates that page
  in place rather than duplicating it.
- A fixture run of the run-ledger ingest carries no human gate for the ingest
  step and its gate criteria assert: the new source page exists under
  `vault/wiki/sources/`, its frontmatter carries a `summary:` line, the
  `vault/wiki/index.md` entry exists, and a `vault/wiki/log.md` entry was
  appended.
- The run-ledger source resolves under `vault/raw/`, never
  `.pi/council/runs/`.
- The repo's typecheck, the full test suite, `python3 council/validate.py`,
  and the repo's preflight pass.
