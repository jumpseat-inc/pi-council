## Intent

The follow-up decision is not worth shipping without a safe failure. This card
reuses the card gate's pinned model, endpoint, and fail-closed transport, and
pins the failure posture at the module boundary: any refusal, timeout, non-2xx,
unparseable body, or unresolved credential records a `gate call failed:` basis
and falls back to the human confirm gate — never dropping a candidate, never
auto-merging one, never writing before confirmation.

## Acceptance

- The call uses `GATE_PINNED_MODEL` and `GATE_ENDPOINT` (never a
  chat/completions path) — one pin, one endpoint, asserted by a test importing
  the constants rather than re-declaring literals.
- An unreachable-endpoint fixture asserts the posture at the module boundary —
  the recorded `gate call failed: <reason>` basis, exactly zero `Drop`
  dispositions, no automatic `Merge`, and the human-confirm fallback — all
  provable offline today; the end-to-end "reaches step 13" arm belongs to
  EV-84's third arm.
- No pathology of the call produces an automatic `Merge` or any write before
  confirmation.
- Availability/credential resolution reuses the resolver the run-start
  preflight already uses; under `gate.mode` on with no credential the run fails
  loud at run start exactly as the card gate does (the FLLWUP-89–92 class, not
  a fork).
- The repo's typecheck, the full test suite, `python3 council/validate.py`, and
  the repo's preflight pass.
