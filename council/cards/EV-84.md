## Intent

The claim "the review is decided by Jev" has to be falsifiable end to end. This
card ships the opt-in falsifier: a run surfacing three candidates (two close,
one new) through the decision, with the fixture pinning `overrides` so the
exact-two-card assertion is model-independent and the arm reads as a wiring
falsifier — candidate → decision → write → ledger — rather than evidence of
Jev's judgment quality. Two further arms cover the off mode and the
unreachable endpoint, each asserting the human pre-write confirm and zero cards
before approval.

## Acceptance

- The falsifier is opt-in (network/gated); the default suite stays green
  without it, and it is the only new network-touching arm.
- With `gate.mode` `active` and a resolvable credential, the fixture pins the
  follow-up policy's `overrides` in a fixture-local
  `council/gate/followup/decision.json` (resolved through EV-78's whole-file
  first-hit path, never an edit to packaged thresholds) so the two dispositions
  are forced and the exact-two-card assertion is model-independent; it asserts
  exactly two cards are written, the merged card's `## Merged from:` names both
  sources, and the ledger carries one line per candidate with a `File` or
  `Merge` disposition.
- With `gate.mode` `off`, the same fixture asserts the step-13 prose confirm
  fires — carrying the unconditional dedup pass — and nothing is written before
  approval.
- With the endpoint unreachable, the same fixture asserts the human fallback
  fires, zero `Drop` dispositions are recorded, and nothing is written before
  approval.
- Red-at-base evidence is recorded per the repo's `red-base evidence`
  convention.
- The repo's typecheck, the full test suite, `python3 council/validate.py`, and
  the repo's preflight pass.
