## Intent

An upgraded consumer must never be silently de-enabled nor have enablement
silently preserved; the fail-loud line names the deprecated file and its
replacement (the EV-62 `FAIL:` surface).

## Acceptance

- A `mode`-bearing `policy.json` produces the named `FAIL:`.
- A tuning-only `policy.json` resolves unchanged.
- The packaged default stays `off`; no auto-migration writes a consumer's
  committed file.
