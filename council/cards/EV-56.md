## Intent

/council-setup is a new Council command and the standing discipline treats a Council command without an end-to-end falsifier as a defect. This is the falsifier for the non-clobber half of the handoff, running through the existing SMOKE_PHASE selector.

## Acceptance

Phase passes on an initialized repo; a deliberately injected write makes it fail.
