## Intent

This is rollout step 4: for reversible, small-blast-radius cards whose
disagreement a runnable check can settle, the routing becomes automatic and the
deliberation panel is skipped. The card's execution still runs the owner and the
judge's fresh-context goal evaluation, because those are what make a merged
change trustworthy; it does not run the branch-verification dispatch, the
independent first pass, the exchange rounds, the attack round, or the synthesis.
The merge check's criteria become mode-aware in the same change, so a Verify card
is judged by the criteria its mode actually produces rather than by criteria the
mode was defined to skip.

The card consumes a recorded decision rather than recomputing one. The verdict
was computed against the card's packed state at intake, keyed by that state's
hash; if the card text is unchanged the recorded decision is the decision, and
re-calling the model would be spending tokens to re-derive a deterministic
function of frozen text. A changed card state has no matching record and takes
the full path.

The falsifier is the point of the card: the mode is only real if the dispatch
set differs observably. The manifest record from the previous card is what makes
that checkable.

## Acceptance

A card routed to Verify has zero deliberation dispatch in its run manifests and
exactly one owner dispatch and one goal evaluation, with no branch-verification
dispatch; the routing decision is read from the ledger record matching the card's
state hash with no gate call issued for unchanged card text; a card whose text
changed after the recorded decision routes to the full mode; and the reduced
panel never omits an adversary or a ruling authority where one is required. A
card whose recorded mode is `Verify` is re-checked at dispatch against the
observed touched-file set, and any hard override that fires on the observed state
runs the full path with a ledger line recording the re-route;
`council/procedures/council.md` step 1 defers to a recorded mode when one
exists, and the procedure text is amended in the same change to name which of
the two is authoritative.
