## Intent

This card is the reachability gate EV-40 sits on. The extension listens to
`agent_settled` (`extensions/index.ts:379`), not `agent_end`. If both branches
return no, EV-40's intent becomes a different card — the intake's surface
becomes a "cannot recover, restart" copy string that must be carved as a new
card by the epic's owner.

## Acceptance

The falsifier runs on the shipped tree and reports, per branch, whether a
second assistant message arrives — the TUI observable is an input-bar text
delta, the headless observable is the stdout line announcing the wait.
