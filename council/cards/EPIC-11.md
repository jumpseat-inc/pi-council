## Intent

/council-init scaffolds a consumer repository mechanically and blind: it installs the council's dependencies and copies the scaffold tree, seeding .council.json with the council's own packaged defaults, so a consumer receives the council's opinions with no fit to their codebase, stack, or surfaces. This epic adds /council-setup, a model-run procedure that grounds itself in the consuming repository before asking anything, interviews the human with a recommendation on every question, and writes a fitted configuration through a code-validated, consent-gated path — while the council's core opinions stay frozen: seat convictions modeled on a real person, the <repository_grounding> block in every seat, a per-seat intelligence tier, and model diversity across the roster.

The recut makes that interview Jev-aware. EPIC-13 shipped the typed `typesafe/jev-1.13` decisions gate; EPIC-14 re-homed its enablement into the committed `.council.json`'s reserved top-level `gate` section (`off`/`advisory`/`active`, toggled by `/council-gate`) and pinned the one resolver. `/council-setup` adds a third decision domain beside the card-dispatch and follow-up domains: a `council/gate/setup/` question-set + decision-policy pair, resolved whole-file first-hit like its siblings, sharing the one `gate.mode` enable surface and the one pinned transport, whose decision is whether the repository keeps the packaged default seat scaffold or redefines it. For each retained seat, the interview asks which real human persona the seat models for the repository, written only as a `seat-emphasis` note — never by forking a packaged body. Jev changes whose decision applies, never whether the human is asked: its disposition is the recommended option at the existing interview question, and the human confirms or edits at the existing draft-then-confirm gate. No new gate, no `Needs Human` stop.

The fail-closed posture is load-bearing and stated, because the live decisions API is currently degraded — `FLLWUP-104` records that `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917` returns noul answers as `{"type":"noul","noul":<p>}` while the engine reads `probability`, so every live card-gate and follow-up call currently fails closed with `invalid-response` (`Deliberate` / `File`). With `gate.mode` off or any call failure, the seat-composition recommendation resolves the literal `Default` — the packaged scaffold — and zero custom seats or emphasis notes are written without an explicit consent act. The safe direction is the council's own tested opinions, never a custom roster manufactured from a failed call; the Jev-aware path is exercised by injected transports and offline fixtures until `FLLWUP-104` lands.

## Acceptance

The epic is complete when the `/council-setup` interview obtains the seat-composition decision from the Jev setup domain with the human asked regardless of its disposition; any unavailable, refused, or failed call — including `gate.mode` off and the live `invalid-response` drift — resolves the recommended disposition to the literal `Default` and writes zero custom seats or seat-emphasis notes without an explicit consent act; a redefined roster keeps every frozen-roster seat and is otherwise refused by the validator with the specific violation literal named; every disposition the setup gate can produce is exactly `Default` or `Redefine`, and the `Redefine` arm is reached by an asserted offline fixture — the epic is not complete if only the fail-closed path works; the recommendation render distinguishes `gate.mode` off from a failed Jev call by its basis, and the `Mode: <disposition> — <basis>` render is presented, never written to any card file; no packaged seat conviction body is modified or forked; `<repository_grounding>` is present in every seat; every write is preceded by explicit consent and a timestamped backup; and re-running `/council-setup` changes nothing that already matches.
