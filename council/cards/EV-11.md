## Intent

/council bounds its deliberation — a fixed round cap, a convergence state,
and a deterministic stop so a session cannot exchange forever. Once EV-10
puts seats into the decomposition, that same bound is required here — the
maintainer asked for the session to be bounded similar to the /council
command, and an unbounded multi-seat exchange would make /features-new
cost- and time-unpredictable at exactly the moment it feeds
/features-deliver's unattended runs.

This card adds the bounds to the rewritten procedure text — a stated round
cap, a convergence criterion (what the facilitator treats as settled enough
to draft), and a defined fallback when the cap is hit without convergence
(the facilitator drafts anyway and carries the open disagreements into the
step-3 draft pass for the human to settle).

Open questions for the council — the cap value (council.md's own cap is the
natural precedent), whether the fallback draft is written by the
facilitator alone or by the most-advanced seat position, and whether
non-convergence should be surfaced to the human as a distinct warning.

User-visible surface — none beyond the draft pass; the bound shows up as
run behavior and, when hit, as a note in the presented draft. Depends on
EV-10.

## Acceptance

- The procedure text states a numeric round cap, a convergence criterion,
  and a fallback outcome for non-convergence.
- The stated bound matches the deliberation bounds of council.md in shape
  (fixed cap, explicit stop) rather than inventing a new scheme.
- python3 council/validate.py stays green; step 3's gate text unchanged.
