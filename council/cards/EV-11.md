## Intent

/council bounds its deliberation — a fixed round cap, a convergence state,
and a deterministic stop so a session cannot exchange forever. Once EV-10
puts seats into the decomposition, that same bound is required here — the
maintainer asked for the session to be bounded similar to the /council
command, and an unbounded multi-seat exchange would make /features-new
cost- and time-unpredictable at exactly the moment it feeds
/features-deliver's unattended runs.

This card adds the bounds to the rewritten procedure text — a stated round
cap, a convergence criterion (what the facilitator treats as settled enough
to draft), and a defined fallback when the cap is hit without convergence
(the facilitator drafts anyway and carries the open disagreements into the
step-3 draft pass for the human to settle).

Open questions for the council — the cap value (council.md's own cap is the
natural precedent), whether the fallback draft is written by the
facilitator alone or by the most-advanced seat position, and whether
non-convergence should be surfaced to the human as a distinct warning.

User-visible surface — none beyond the draft pass; the bound shows up as
run behavior and, when hit, as a note in the presented draft. Depends on
EV-10.

Wave 1 = round 1, wave 2 = round 2 (its two parallel dispatches are *one* round — the text must say this, or a dispatch-counting facilitator miscounts at 4), wave 3 = round 3. > **Convergence criterion.** The session has converged when wave 3 has ruled every open-judgment dispute the attackers surfaced — epic goal and each child's `state` ratified or amended, each dispute ruled with dissent named — so that the aggregation's disagreement ledger contains only **named dissents** (seats that did not endorse), never unruled disputes. Non-convergence's concrete instance is product-owner escalation (its `<escalation>` forbids portfolio change, reversing a recorded human decision, or a defective goal; per SEATS-1 that escalation goes to the human). >

The wave-3-amended draft naturally dominates the aggregation anyway — but as a property of the aggregation, not a facilitator choice. The fallback changes only the ledger content, never the wave structure and never Part 1: the unresolved items reuse the existing `Decision: unresolved — your call` markers (line 114's ledger contract), and the cap-hit note is a Part 2 line, never Part 1 card text (the attribution-free mandate at line 127 is untouched).

The card's own user-visible surface says "when hit, as a note in the presented draft". So: one line at the top of the ledger surface — the session did not converge within its round cap; the listed disagreements are unresolved and require the human's decision. Presentation-only, never written to disk, never a new gate.

> - **No exchange loop at all** — a re-dispatch exchange could in principle sharpen the decomposition, but it is what the card's goal forbids (cost/time unpredictability feeding `/features-deliver`'s unattended runs) and what the EV-10 seam explicitly excludes.
