## Intent

The preflight `FAIL:` is the surface; it names the condition's consumer and the
remediation (set the env var **or** use the stored `api_key`), never a
symptom-only message.

## Acceptance

- A named `FAIL:` under enabled + no-credential, naming the decisions gate and
  both remediations.
- Off-mode output unchanged; the existing unconditional check remains.
- A stored credential satisfies it (runtime parity).
- The mode comes from `loadGateConfig`, not a shell re-parse.
- The fresh-scaffold-only reach is stated on the card.
