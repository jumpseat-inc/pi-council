## Intent

Today steps 1 and 2 of council/procedures/features-new.md are executed by
the facilitator solo — one agent reads the template and board, slices the
children, drafts every goal, and presents the result. This card rewrites
those steps so the decomposition is deliberated — dispatched seats
(council_dispatch + council_wait, already available to the parent session,
no engine change) contribute to the slicing, the goal sentences, the state
assignments, and the designer-surface flags in each child's Intent, and the
facilitator aggregates their input into the single draft pass step 3
presents.

"Interested agents" is deliberately left as the deliberation's first open
question — the seats should rule on which seats participate (all nine, a
default subset, or a content-driven selection rule) and how a seat's
disagreement is carried into the draft pass rather than silently resolved
by the facilitator.

User-visible surface — the human's draft pass itself; the set of cards
presented at step 3 now carries seat attributions and named disagreements.
No TUI, no copy strings; whether this warrants a designer seat is the
council's call, flagged here explicitly.

The draft-then-confirm gate is not part of this card's surface — step 3's
text (write nothing to disk until the human approves) must survive this
rewrite verbatim.

## Acceptance

- The rewritten procedure text mandates seat dispatch for the decomposition
  steps and states how contributions and disagreements reach the step-3
  draft pass.
- A smoke run of /features-new on a toy feature shows the seats actually
  dispatched (job ids on record) and the presented draft naming them.
- Step 3's draft-then-confirm text is byte-identical to before the change;
  python3 council/validate.py stays green.

## Phase 1 rulings (recorded 2026 by the human before the run, immutable for this run, binding on every seat including steward)

- **Ruling SEATS-1 (interested agents):** the seats that participate in a
  /features-new decomposition are **product-owner, designer, principal, and
  skeptic**. If the product-owner requires it, escalation goes to the CEO
  (the human), reached through the orchestrator. No other seats deliberate
  by default; the procedure must name this set explicitly.
- **Ruling SMOKE-1 (smoke-run hygiene):** the acceptance smoke run executes
  /features-new against a **scratch copy of council/** (temp directory),
  never the real board. Evidence for the dispatched-job-ids criterion is
  the job ids captured from that scratch run. The real council/board.md is
  not modified by any smoke run.
