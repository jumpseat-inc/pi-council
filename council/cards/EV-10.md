## Intent

This is the "where to start" answer and the epic's design authority. The
Council deliberates and settles the definitions every later child builds on:
what "accurately measure" means for a multi-step agent command (gates green?
goal met? a judge verdict? cost and latency?) and for a single seat turn;
the harness architecture; how a model id is injected per run without
editing committed files; the rubric shape; and the confidence method (how
many repeats per cell, what variance means, when a difference is real).

Produces `docs/superpowers/specs/<date>-council-eval-design.md` and a
matching plan, self-reviewed for placeholders, internal consistency, scope,
and ambiguity, then committed. Nothing in the spec asserts a design choice
as settled that the deliberation left open.

Not surface-touching (documentation; no designer seat).

## Acceptance

- Spec + plan files exist under docs/superpowers/ and define the five items
  in the goal with falsifiable acceptance criteria each.
- The spec records the model-injection mechanism EV-11 implements, the
  fixture schema EV-12 fills, the rubric EV-13 grades by, and the matrix and
  variance semantics EV-14/EV-15 rely on.
- `python3 council/validate.py` still passes after the docs edits.
