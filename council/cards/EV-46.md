## Intent

Step 14 of `council/procedures/council.md` asks the facilitator to "Offer to
file anything durable from this run … into the wiki via `/wiki-ingest`" and
then to stop: the offer is a prompt, the human may decline, and nothing
records whether the run's learnings were ever filed. The ask here is that the
ingest happens, every time, without an offer.

The seam is not `council.md` alone. `council/procedures/council.md` is the
contract both execution modes read: an attended facilitator runs its steps
directly, and a card container in an autonomous epic runs "council.md's steps
under those substitutions". So a bare "just do the ingest" edit to step 14
lands on both paths at once, and the container cannot satisfy it as written:
it may not dispatch the ruling seats, its only durable outputs are the card
file and the board, and its report is one of exactly four tags — ESCALATION,
DONE, RETIRED, HALT — none of which carries an ingest outcome. A container
that ignored the gap could hand-edit `vault/`, which is the act step 14
itself forbids.

This card closes the gap for single-card runs in both modes: the attended
completion performs the run-ledger ingest instead of offering it, and the
container path either performs it or returns the ingest's judgment question
through the escalation channel it already has, resuming with the ruling. What
the container may never do silently is drop the ingest or write `vault/`
outside the operation.

This card is Backlog until the run-ledger ingest capability is settled — it
wires a call to an operation whose write owner, artifact, and escalation
target are that capability's output.

Surface: the run-completion line a person reads (what was ingested, or that a
question is outstanding), the amend affordance, and the `## Provenance` note;
an attended run loses its step-14 offer prompt from the interaction.

## Acceptance

- `council/procedures/council.md` step 14 performs the run-ledger ingest
  rather than offering it, while keeping the existing prohibition on
  hand-editing `vault/`.
- `council/agents/council-runner.md` carries the container-side obligation:
  perform the ingest at completion, or return the ingest's judgment question
  as an `ESCALATION` report and resume with the ruling. Its
  `<return_contract>` carries the ingest outcome on the terminal report
  (`DONE`, and `RETIRED` where the card is withdrawn) so the orchestrator can
  observe it.
- A container that cannot complete the ingest never silently drops it and
  never writes `vault/` outside the operation's own steps.
- The run-completion line names what was ingested (pages created, pages
  updated, contradictions flagged) in copy that follows the wiki schema's own
  vocabulary.
- A `test/hub.test.ts`-style runner dispatch carrying a card whose ingest
  needs a judgment call returns `ESCALATION` with the question in the report;
  a runner that silently drops the ingest, or hand-writes `vault/` outside
  the operation, or reports `DONE` without an ingest outcome, fails the test.
- Every page autonomously ingested carries frontmatter `provenance:
  run-ledger` plus a `## Provenance` note (`auto-ingested from
  <run-ledger-id>`); the wiki-ingest procedure gains a follow-up variant that
  amends an auto-ingested page, reachable from the closed card's board row.
- The run-completion line names what was ingested in the wiki schema's
  vocabulary and mirrors on the board (the closed card's `Done` row gains an
  `ingested → vault/raw/.../run-ledger.md` pointer) and on the wiki source
  page (frontmatter `provenance: run-ledger`).
- A fixture run of a completed card carries no human gate at the ingest step
  and carries a gate asserting the run's source page under
  `vault/wiki/sources/`; the fixture's existing step-11 merge gate is
  unaffected.
- The repo's typecheck, the full test suite, `python3 council/validate.py`,
  and the repo's preflight pass.
