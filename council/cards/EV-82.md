## Intent

Surface: the `/council` step-13 confirm-gate chat line, and the absence of an
unqualified decision when the gate is off. Step 13 today offers the drafts for
edit, drop, or approval with no typed decision. This card records the decision
and renders it — a sign, not a tag, naming the candidate, the disposition, the
consequence (merge target or drop), and the live mode — at the pre-write
confirm gate, with the unavailable states rendered in the `FLLWUP-75` shape.
The board/open-card/sibling dedup pass is unconditional: `gate.mode` switches
*whose decision is applied*, never *whether the reader is asked to look at the
board*. The pre-write confirmation pin lives here.

## Acceptance

- Step 13 names the recorded decision and the three enablement states; `off`
  leaves an unqualified human review that still carries the unconditional
  board/open-card/sibling dedup pass, `advisory` renders without enforcing,
  `active` applies the disposition after confirmation, and no mode writes
  before confirmation.
- The rendered line is a sign, not a tag: it names the candidate's title, the
  disposition, the consequence — the `Merge` target id (board card or same-run
  sibling) for `Merge`, the candidate's own title for `Drop` — the live mode
  qualifier, and the basis, on one line continuous with the shipped
  `Mode: <disposition> — <basis>` grammar.
- A `Merge` writes `## Merged from:` on the merged-into card, naming both
  source candidates, plus the chat line naming the target.
- Unavailable-state render, in the `FLLWUP-75` shape: one literal line per
  unavailable state (`off`; `gate call failed: <reason>`; credential
  unresolved; model-card coming-soon), replaced not appended, and zero lines
  when the gate resolves — no deliberation, failure, or verdict wording beyond
  the exact literals.
- The rendered line is presented, never written: a test asserts the full
  `Mode: <disposition> — <basis>` render appears in no card file.
- EV-82 owns the pre-write pin: a prose test asserts the literal
  `confirmed at ledger level` appears only inside a negating sentence, so step
  13 states confirmation precedes any card write; the acceptance names
  `FLLWUP-69`, whose halves stay its own (`Backlog`, `epic: EPIC-9`).
- A mid-run `gate.mode` flip is visible to the next read (the
  run-config-stability class the card gate carries).
- The repo's typecheck, the full test suite, `python3 council/validate.py`, and
  the repo's preflight pass.

### Round 1 — designer (first pass, job-11.3)

**Design position.** The disposition line at step 13 is
`Mode: <File|Merge|Drop> — <candidate-title>[ → <target-id>] (<advisory|active>): <basis>`
— one line per candidate, sitting directly under its drafted card (per-candidate
attachment mirroring EV-67's `council_gate_render`), carrying the five mandatory
constituents (candidate identity, disposition, target-or-cost, live mode qualifier,
basis) with `Mode: <disposition> — ` preserved as the continuous substring of
`decisionLine` (`extensions/gate-ledger.ts:234`). The four unavailable-state lines are
R8's exact literals, sitting above the drafted cards in the FLLWUP-75 positional slot
(replaced not appended, zero lines when the gate resolves). The pre-write pin and the
unconditional dedup-pass sentence sit above the drafts in every mode including `off`.
In `off`, the disposition lines are absent and the `off` literal names the state
without drama; the `(advisory)` vs `(active)` qualifier on the disposition line is the
only thing that makes "this is a recommendation" and "this is what confirmation
applies" unmistakable, and `Mode: <disposition> — <basis>` is the spine both modes
share.

**Byte order details.** `<candidate-title>` = the card's title; deterministic fallback
to the card id when the title exceeds 60 chars (owner pins). `<target-id>` = the merge
target's id for `Merge`; absent for `File`; absent for `Drop` (the cost is the
candidate's own title, already on the line). `(<advisory|active>)` = the live mode
qualifier. `<basis>` = the verbatim basis string from the gate's recorded decision —
never prose authored at render time. Examples, advisory:
`Mode: File — Re-ingest gate ledger (advisory): low noul`;
`Mode: Merge — Re-ingest gate ledger → EV-90 (advisory): exact duplicate`;
`Mode: Merge — Re-ingest gate ledger → FLLWUP-92 (advisory): overlapping scope`;
`Mode: Drop — Re-ingest gate ledger (advisory): out of scope`. Active: same lines with
`(active)` swapped in. For two-source merges, each candidate gets its own disposition
line naming the target via `→ <target-id>`; the merged-into card gains
`## Merged from:` naming both sources.

Unavailable-state lines (R8 exact literals; replaced not appended; zero lines when the
gate resolves): `off`; `gate call failed: <reason>`; `credential unresolved`;
`model-card coming-soon`. Positional shape (step-13 chat transcript, top to bottom):
(1) pre-write pin prose (R0b); (2) dedup pass sentence — unconditional, every mode
(exact wording the owner's; scope board/open-cards/siblings is the designer's);
(3) unavailable-state line, if and only if the gate is unavailable; (4) `## Drafts`
heading; (5) per-candidate: drafted card markdown, then disposition line directly
under it.

**The `off`-mode experience.** A person in `off` mode reads: pre-write pin + dedup
pass + `off` literal + `## Drafts` + drafted cards (NO per-candidate disposition
lines). The absence of the disposition line is the right sign. Minimum text for the
`off` state: the bare literal `off`; no expanding sentence (taste, flagged).

**advisory vs active as a person experiences it.** Same line format, same spine, same
basis, same target/cost; only the mode qualifier differs. In `(advisory)`, the
disposition line is informational — ignoring a `Merge`/`Drop` recommendation turns the
candidate into a `File`. In `(active)`, confirmation applies the disposition: `File`
writes the card; `Merge` writes `## Merged from:` on the target card and no new card
for the source (R7); `Drop` writes no card. The pre-write pin applies in both modes
(R0b). The line is presented, never written.

**Falsifiable predictions (skeptic-runnable smokes, never gate assertions):**
1. Target-id gap — a person reading `Mode: Merge — Re-ingest gate ledger (advisory):
   exact duplicate` cannot name the merge target from the line alone. Smoke: cold-read
   persona, "where does this merge to?" — settles the `→ <target-id>` form.
2. Mode qualifier is the smallest mapping fix — advisory and active produce
   byte-identical text *modulo* the qualifier; if fully byte-identical, Finding C
   stands and the qualifier is missing.
3. Off-mode silently swallows the disposition lines — if per-candidate disposition
   lines appear under `off` (rather than the `off` literal + drafted cards only), the
   FLLWUP-75 shape is broken.
4. The line is written, not presented — `grep -rln` the disposition-line pattern over
   `council/cards/`; any hit breaks the pin.
5. Drop's cost is invisible — if the candidate title does not suffice as the visible
   cost, a cost marker is needed.
6. Double `gate:` colon — `gate call failed:` must not render as
   `gate: gate call failed: <reason>`; a guard against over-eager namespace
   normalization.

**Preferences, ranked last (taste, not settled):** id-vs-title asymmetry (title-first
wins; 60-char threshold to pin); `↔` for sibling merges vs `→` for all (leans `→` for
consistency); no `gate:` namespace prefix on the three prefix-less unavailable-state
