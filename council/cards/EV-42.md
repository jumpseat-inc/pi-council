## Intent

Each attempt currently mints a fresh job id, a fresh manifest, and a fresh
session file, so the tree shows N dispatches for one intent while the subtree
usage silently absorbs N attempts' tokens — two slices each assuming the other
owns the distinction. The card must name the manifest field (an `attempt`
integer plus a stable `attemptGroupId` binding sibling attempts together) and
the tree-row and usage-block copy that carries the distinction.
