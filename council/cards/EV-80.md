## Intent

The card gate's state packer cannot answer the merge question: it packs a
card's touched files and tests, which do not exist at draft time, and never
loads the board or the run's other candidates. This card ships the follow-up
packer that gives the decision what it needs — the candidate, every open card,
and the run's not-yet-drafted siblings — as explicit, deterministic,
budget-bounded input with a declared drop record.

## Acceptance

- The packer resolves fixed declared sections (candidate, board/open-card
  targets, same-run siblings) with per-section caps against the policy's
  `gateStateBudgetTokens`; over-budget state is cut at the tail and recorded as
  a `DropRecord`.
- It reads the board through `council/board.md` and each open card file, and
  never reads `.pi/council/runs/` (the accessor-discipline test idiom).
- Determinism: the same inputs yield byte-identical `stateBytes`/`stateHash`; a
  test mutates a board card and a sibling and asserts the hash changes, then
  mutates an unrelated file and asserts it does not.
- It takes the board path and sibling list as explicit inputs — never an
  ambient git diff or uncommitted board state.
- The repo's typecheck, the full test suite, `python3 council/validate.py`, and
  the repo's preflight pass.

- **Determinism:** `stateBytes = TextEncoder().encode(JSON.stringify(state))`,

- **Test plan** (`test/ev80-followup-state.test.ts`, tmpdir fixture, EV-75-clean):

not opaque bytes+hash alone. Pin packed entry shapes: open card `{id, title, goal}`,

**Blind spots named:** (1) no `FollowupState` return/export contract in the acceptance;
