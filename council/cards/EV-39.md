## Intent

This card does not address the intake; it fixes a child seat's provider error.
Council already owns this path — the child's error is captured at
`hub.ts:196-205` and the parent can re-spawn the job — so it is the second,
easier half of the epic.

The budget source is the `retry` section's `maxAttempts`, `baseDelayMs`,
`maxDelayMs`, and `jitter` fields (R2), and the delay follows pi's existing
shape (`baseDelayMs * 2^(attempt-1)`, capped at `maxDelayMs`, jittered when
`jitter` is true). The final report carries an explicit attempts sentence.

## Orchestrator rulings (Phase 1, binding)

Recorded human decisions for this run. Immutable and binding on every seat,
`steward` included; a runner that hits one applies it and cites the ruling
rather than re-asking.

- **R4 (intermediate-attempt visibility).** One job-tree row per dispatch,
  labeled `attempt 2/3` while retrying — no extra rows and no separate status
  line, so the tree never shows two rows for one intent. The final report
  still names the attempt count.
