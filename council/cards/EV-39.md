## Intent

This card does not address the intake; it fixes a child seat's provider error.
Council already owns this path — the child's error is captured at
`hub.ts:196-205` and the parent can re-spawn the job — so it is the second,
easier half of the epic.

The card must still pin the budget source (the `retry` section's field names:
`maxAttempts`, `baseDelayMs`, `maxDelayMs`, `jitter`), the delay formula (base,
factor, jitter, and whether the wait is a real sleep or an injected clock), and
the exact attempts sentence carried on the final report. The open decision for
the Council is what the human sees on the intermediate attempts — silence, a
status line, or a transcript row.
