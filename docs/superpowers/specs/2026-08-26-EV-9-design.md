## Scope (settled)

Pressing **Enter** on the selected (highlighted) row of the EV-7 inline
below-editor job tree opens that subagent's **live progress view** — the
same streaming transcript content the modal viewer (`TranscriptView`)
shows today — rendered **inline** as an expansion of the tree panel's
region, per Phase 1 ruling R1. Closing the progress view returns focus to
the tree with the selection preserved; tree last-activity rows keep
updating while progress is open. Progress must render inline (no modal, no
`withModalFrame`, no full-screen overlay), and the tree panel/rows stay
visible above the progress expansion.

## Binding rulings (product-owner, on record — do not reopen)

- **EV-9 R1 (Phase 1, 2026-08-26):** progress renders **inline**, as an
  expansion of the tree panel's region, in the below-editor area — not as
  the full-screen modal overlay. The tree panel and its rows stay visible
  above the progress expansion. The driver comes back to the tree with the
  selection intact.
- **EV-9 viewport-floor (product-owner, this run — do not reopen):**
  min supported terminal height **pinned at 7 rows**. At `termRows ≤ 6`,
  **Enter on a highlighted tree row is consumed as a no-op**:
  `controller.surface` stays `"tree"`, no progress viewport opens, no
  separator row, the tree rows remain visible (`treeLines ≥ 1`). Esc
  continues to do the right thing. At `termRows ≥ 7`, the two-regime
  formula runs as designed. Implemented as a single guard
  `if (termRows < 7) return;` at the top of the controller's
  `enterProgress(sid)`. No new surface value, no new transition, **no
  chrome / separator / floor compression**.
  (Options (b) and the (a) indicator variant were rejected — see the
  ruling's source doc; do not re-litigate them.)

The owner bears a second obligation **not** closed by the ruling: the
formula's **upper-bound invariant violation at `termRows ∈ {7..11}`** (O1's
lower-bound fix is the ruling's; the upper bound was not surfaced by the
consolidator). Section "Viewport layout (two-regime, upper-bound-fixed)"
below addresses it, and the owner must implement it so the whole formula is
consistent, not just the lower bound. Carry this into the tests.

## Settled design

### Vehicle

- **Surface union widening.** `controller.surface` goes from the EV-8
  `"editor" | "tree"` to `"editor" | "tree" | "progress"` — a **discriminated
  union**, not a parallel `progressSessionId` flag/rods. The union is
  additive and monotonic; the EV-8 settled `surface` is the documented sole
  arbiter. The parallel-flag fallback (tree∧flag≠null fourth state) is
  rejected.
- **Same widget, stacked lines.** The below-editor `CouncilTreeWidget`
  region is one component that renders `[...treeLines, separator?, ...viewLines]`
  when progress is open. No second `setWidget`, no `ctx.ui.custom` overlay,
  no `withModalFrame`. Editor stays the TUI's focused component (EV-8
  binding); progress focus = `controller.surface === "progress"`.
- **Enter activates any selected row → progress.** `CustomTreeEditor` calls a
  NEW controller method `enterProgress(sid)` (not `activateSession`/modal).
  NEW controller method `enterProgress(sid)` (not `activateSession`/modal).
  Running or settled, the selected row opens its progress — parity with the
  modal's any-row behavior.
- **Escape restores the tree preserving selection.** `backFromProgress()`
  is a NEW transition that sets `controller.surface = "tree"` and keeps
  `selectedSessionId` unchanged. It must **never** call `exit()` — `exit()`
  nulls `selectedSessionId` (O3 closed-red: naive exit()-based escape would
  destroy selection preservation).
- **Enter inside progress = consumed no-op.** `TranscriptView` has no Enter
  branch; an unconsumed Enter (or a raw newline) must not submit an unsent
  draft in the editor buffer. Enter is classified "`enter`" in the new
  progress key-classifier and consumed (no-op) while in progress.
- **Exhaustive progress-key routing.** A NEW pure `classifyProgressKey(data)`
  in `focus-nav.ts` (or a new module), **not** a surface-parameterized
  re-write of `classifyTreeKey`. `classifyTreeKey` and its 5-way split stay
  byte-identical; the EV-8 test file passes **unmodified**. The tree/editor
  branches of `routeEditorFocus` are byte-identical; a **`progress` branch**
  is added where `surface === "tree"` sites exist (the three
  `surface === "tree"` sites: routeEditorFocus, CustomTreeEditor.render,
  CouncilTreeWidget.render), each gaining a progress branch with a `never`
  default proving exhaustiveness (O2 closed-red: the widening does not
  exist yet — this is the required implementation).
- **Render cache folds the progress predicate.** `CouncilTreeWidget.render`
  memoizes on `sig = surface:selectedSessionId`; the third surface value must
  fold in (O4/principal, the EV-4 cached widget bug class). The cache does
  NOT need to fold a transcript revision counter — the stream update rides
  **`view.onChange → widget.refresh()`** (not just `invalidate()`). O5
  closed-red showed `invalidate()` alone leaves a stale tree **row list** (a
  new job landing during progress must appear). `refresh()` clears cached +
  re-reads rows; same "new job during progress → row appears on escape-back"
  test. Transcript expansion / thinking toggles also fire onChange (the
  fragment lives inside the view behind the hook; no revision-counter in the
  sig — that is what makes the counter the wrong depth).
- **Two independent clocks.** Transcript tail polls on a **1s** timer;
  the tree's last-activity refresh on its existing **2s** timer. Both
  timers are registered in the widget factory and **both are cleared in
  `dispose()`** (plus the 2s tree interval already cleared). A new job or a
  transcript append must reach the screen on the cadence that owns it,
  independently.
- **View ownership / key delivery.** View ownership (the live
  `TranscriptView` instance) lives in the widget; key delivery happens
  through the always-focused editor (pi widgets are render-only —
  Pattern 5; the editor is always-focused OJ-1/EV-8). `CustomEditor`is the
  intersection point: progress keys reach the view via a `viewHost`
  reference the widget installs on the controller.
- **Token-only (AGENTS.md 9.6 + EV-4 settled).** The separator line, the
  progress header line, and the progress transcript lines all draw from
  existing theme tokens (`t.fg("dim", ...)`, `t.bold`, `t.fg("accent",...)`
  etc.); no inline ANSI/hex, no new theme tokens.

### Enter/escape transitions in progress

While `surface === "progress"`, `handleInput(data)`:
- **Esc → `backFromProgress()`**: `surface = "tree"`, selection preserved,
  `requestRender()`. (Unconsumed if not handled; the tree mode returns.)
- **Enter → consume as no-op.** Stays in progress; nothing else changes.
- **Up/Down/e/t/f/g/G** — consumed by the live view (scroll / expand /
  thinking / follow). Delivered to the `TranscriptView`'s `handleInput`;
  the view's own open/close/Escape semantics (per existing TranscriptView)
  apply only while in progress.
- **Every other key** — forward-unhandled to `super/prior.handleInput`
  (the editor buffer), exactly the EV-8 discipline. If the Escape is consumed
  (not forwarded to a subview), the surface returns to tree.

Note: EV-8's `TREE_ROW_MARKER`/`TREE_MODE_LABEL` stays rendered while
`surface === "tree"`; in progress the mode label reflects a progress/exit
line (`-- PROGRESS --` or `<seat> progress · esc back` — the exact string is
the implementer's copy choice, token-only).

### Shared controller (`TreeFocusState` extended)

Add to the existing `TreeFocusState`:
- `openProgress(sessionId: string): void` — guard `if (termRowsCap < 7) return;`
  (termRowsCap = the widget's captured terminal height, see below), else
  set `surface = "progress"`, keep `selectedSessionId = sessionId`.
- `backFromProgress(): void` — if surface is progress, set `surface = "tree"`;
  NEVER `exit()`; never null selection.
- The ruling names the entry `enterProgress(sid)`; use exactly that name for
  the controller method the editor calls. The guard and the no-op semantics
  are what matter: `if (termRowsCap < 7) return;` before any surface
  transition.
- selection resolution stays **sessionId-keyed** (EV-8 O6): `selectedIndex`,
  move, `isAtTop/Bottom` unchanged; `rows` list rebuilt by the widget's
  `refresh()`.

### Viewport computation (two-regime, upper-bound addressed)

Constants: `CHROME = 5`, `SEPARATOR = 1`, `TREE_FLOOR = 3`,
`PROGRESS_FLOOR = 3`, `TREE_MIN_BUDGET = 7`, `ROWS_MAX = 11`.

```
avail = max(1, termRows - CHROME)            // widget rows available below the editor
```

**Normal regime — unchanged (term ≥ 12 where avail ≥ 7):**

```
treeLines = max(TREE_FLOOR, min(ROWS_MAX, treeContentLines, avail - SEPARATOR - PROGRESS_FLOOR))
progressViewport = max(PROGRESS_FLOOR, avail - treeLines - SEPARATOR)
```

Pinned: `(24,8) → (8,10)` → 8+1+10=19=avail(19) ✓ ; `(40,8) → (8,26)` →
8+1+26=35=avail(35) ✓ ; `(12,8) → (3,3)` → 3+1+3=7=avail(7) ✓.

**Tiny regime — term ∈ {7..11} (2 ≤ avail < 7), UPPER-BOUND FIX:**

```
sep = (avail >= 3) ? SEPARATOR : 0         // separator row wears at the hard floor (avail=2)
treeLines = max(1, min(ROWS_MAX, treeContentLines, avail - sep - 1))   // tree anchors; progress floor 1
progressLines = max(1, avail - sep - treeLines)                        // never less than its 1-row floor
```

This is the rule-flagged upper-bound repair: **the original R3 tiny
regime ignored the separator row**, splitting the full `avail` between tree
and progress, so `treeLines + SEPARATOR + progressLines` overflowed `avail`
by one row at every tiny height (e.g. pinned `(8,5) → (1,2)` summed 1+1+2=4 >
avail=3). The fix reserves the separator first (treeBudget), splits the
remainder, and lets the **separator yield** (row omitted) only at `avail <
3` (term 7, `avail=2`), where there is no row room even for tree+progress
both ≥ 1 — preserving the ruling's capacity for **treeLines ≥ 1 ∧
progressLines ≥ 1** at every `termRows ≥ 7`.

Verified across the tiny band (treeContentLines assumed ≥ budget, giving each
row its max tree and a ≥ 1 progress floor; any smaller RC yields fewer treeLines
and more progressLines, the sum pinning to `avail − sep` in all cases):

| termRows | avail | sep | treeLines | progressLines | sum (≤avail) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7  | 2 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 ✓ |
| 8  | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 ✓ |
| 9  | 4 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 4 ✓ |
| 10 | 5 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 5 ✓ |
| 11 | 6 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 6 ✓ |

Rewrite of the violating pinned case: `(8,5) → (1,1)` (1+1+1=3=avail), not the
old `(1,2)` that overflowed. `(12,8)→(3,3)`, `(40,11)→(11,23)` are normal-regime
and unchanged.

Factory invariant (all regimes): `render(width).length ≤ max(1, termRows−5)`;
tree winnowing always keeps the region within `termRows − CHROME`.

**Where the guard lives.** The term-height is captured **once** in the
widget factory where `tui.terminal` is visible (mirroring the modal's
`Math.max(10, (tui.terminal?.rows ?? 24))` at navigator.ts:416/651), never per-render. The
guard is `if (termRows < 7) return;` at the top of
`controller.enterProgress(sid)`; the widget still renders tree rows (EV-7
surface, unchanged) whenever `termRows < 7`, Enter simply never transitions
to `"progress"`.
