- **Status:** settled by the step-2–6 deliberation and the step-6
  `product-owner` ruling (`job-17`), appended verbatim to
  `council/cards/EV-36.md`. This document is the write-up, not a

- **Binding inputs:** Phase 1 rulings **R-COPY** (the one-row line reuses

## 1. Problem

The card's `goal` is: *"At every inline progress viewport from one row to a
full panel the transcript's rendered line count never exceeds the granted
viewport and is never empty, and the one-row case identifies the active block
rather than a mid-body continuation line, proven by render assertions over
the pinned termRows 7, 8, 9 and 40 layout vectors."* The deliberation and the
Skeptic's step-4 runs established four facts, each with a real probe result:

1. **The content half fails today.** In follow mode
   `topLine = all.length − viewportRows` (`navigator.ts:814-815`). At a
   one-row grant the single visible line is the last flattened line. A
   trailing `user`/`assistant` unit always renders its body
   (`unitLines`' `showBody`, `navigator.ts:725-732`) and that body wraps, so
   the floor line is a wrapped continuation with no block identity.
   Skeptic **O1** `closed-red`: `user oneRow= ["many words yes"]`,
   `assistant oneRow= ["here"]`, `toolResult oneRow= ["  odata"]`.
2. **The grant can shrink under a cached view.** `ensureView` returns the
   cached view when the sessionId matches and **ignores its
   `viewportRows` argument** (`navigator.ts:438-440`); `viewportRows` is a
   constructor field (`:628`, set at `:445`). A second job landing mid-progress
   drops `computeProgressLayout(9, 2).progressLines` from 2 to 1
   (`focus-nav.ts:105-115`) while the view keeps `viewportRows = 2`; the
   outer `slice(0, layout.progressLines)` (`:432`) masks the *count* but not
   the *window*, and the served line is a mid-body continuation. Skeptic
   **O4** `closed-red` (`layout922 progressLines=1`, `view.viewportRows(after)=2`,
   line `"  streaming output line with many words …"`). termRows 9 is one of
   the card's own pinned acceptance vectors.
3. **A `single`-kind head is untruncated.** The toolCall composed head is
   `truncateToWidth`'d (`:708`) but the `single` head is emitted raw
   (`:723`, `const out = [marker + head]`). Skeptic **O6** `closed-red`: an
   unpaired `toolResult` with a 60-char label renders `"▌ ⎿ aaaa… · 2b"` at
   69 cells in a 40-cell width, wrapping to two physical rows the logical
   count half cannot see.
4. **The logical count half and never-empty already hold.** Skeptic **O5**
   `closed-green` (`grant=1 rendered=1` at `(7,5)`, `(8,5)`, `(9,11)`;
   `grant=23 rendered=4` at `(40,11)`), **O10** `closed-green`
   (`(waiting for output · idle)` at grant 1 over an empty file), **O12**
   `closed-green` (the four pinned pairs reproduce exactly). These are kept
   as regression guards.

The card's other settled facts: the projection must live **view-side** in
`TranscriptView.render` (all three generators converged; a widget-side
projection forks the composed-head construction and breaks T11 byte-parity
`test/ev9-progress.test.ts:309-320`); head semantics for non-toolCall units
come free from `unitLines` element 0 (Skeptic **O8** head-definitions
`closed-green`); the `single`-kind marker is **already** `isFocused`-gated
(Skeptic **O7** `closed-green`), so no companion marker change is needed;
T11 parity holds today at grant 1 and the modal can never reach grant 1
(Skeptic **O9** premise `closed-green`, `G5` modalVp `6/20/36`); and
`computeProgressLayout` is untouched.

## 2. Settled design

### 2.1 The projection subject — R3 (ruling Q1)

At a one-row grant, `TranscriptView.render` returns **exactly one line**: the
composed head (element 0 of `unitLines`) of the **effective-indexed unit**,
computed **read-only** as:

```
effective = this.follow ? vis.length - 1 : fv
```

where `vis` is `this.visible()` and `fv` is the existing clamped
`this.focused` (`navigator.ts:797-798`). `focused` is **never written** by the
projection — the only render-time write that remains is the existing
top-only range clamp at `:797-798`, which the EV-35 Q5 ruling explicitly
carved out as the one clamp added. `isFocused` stays the existing `vi === fv`
expression (`:709`), so:

- **`follow` off** → `effective === fv`: the line is the cursor's unit and
  carries `▌`. The marker and `e` coincide.
- **`follow` on, `vis.length === 1`** → `effective === fv === 0`: the line is
  the cursor's unit and carries `▌`.
- **`follow` on, `vis.length > 1`** → `effective = vis.length − 1 ≠ fv`
  (fresh view: `fv = 0`): the line is the live tail's composed head and
  carries **no `▌`**. This is the shipped comprehension rule (ruling Q3, and
  the card `Intent` amendment): *marker = cursor, no marker = watching the
  live tail*. The marker reappears on the next navigation keypress because
  `up`/`down`/`g`/`G` set `follow = false` (`navigator.ts:766-779`). The cost
  is one `e` press at fresh view that is silent (the body is sliced out by the
  one-row grant), recoverable by widening.

R1 (`starts[focused]`, ignore follow) is rejected: at a fresh view it pins the
oldest unit's head while the JSONL streams past. R2 (`this.focused =
vis.length − 1` in `render` at vp 1 + follow) is rejected: it is
cursor-stability work, which EV-35's Q5 anti-goal forbids, and the step-6
ruling's Q2 confirms Q5 reaches it.

Shape of the change inside `render` (the existing `vis`/`all`/`starts`
construction is otherwise unchanged):

```ts
const maxTop = Math.max(0, all.length - this.viewportRows);
if (this.viewportRows === 1) {
  // EV-36 (R3): one-row floor — the effective-indexed unit's composed head,
  // read-only. follow ? live tail : cursor. No write to `focused`.
  const line = vis.length === 0 ? all[1]! : all[starts[this.follow ? vis.length - 1 : fv]!]!;
  return [line];
}
if (this.follow) this.topLine = maxTop;
else this.topLine = Math.min(Math.max(0, focusLine - 2), maxTop);
return all.slice(this.topLine, this.topLine + this.viewportRows);
```

`all[1]` is the existing empty-vis placeholder because the
`vis.length === 0` branch (`:801`) pushes exactly one line when `vis` is
empty. The projection returns early, so it does not touch `this.topLine`; when
the grant later grows ≥ 2 the slice branch recomputes `topLine` on every
render, so no staleness carries.

### 2.2 The grant is a per-render parameter (ruling Q4 context, frozen-grant fix)

The projection keys on the **current** grant, so the grant must reach the
cached view. `TranscriptView` gains a one-line mutator:

```ts
setViewportRows(n: number): void {
  this.viewportRows = Math.max(1, n);
}
```

and `renderProgress` re-grants on **every** render, fresh or cached, before
the view renders (`navigator.ts:431-432`):

```ts
const view = this.controller?.selectedSessionId ? this.ensureView(layout.progressLines) : undefined;
if (view) view.setViewportRows(layout.progressLines);
const viewLines = view ? view.render(width).slice(0, layout.progressLines) : [];
```

`ensureView`'s early return (`:438-440`) is left as-is; the re-grant is the
single seam that fixes it. The modal path (`openTranscript`, `:830+`)
constructs its own `TranscriptView` and never calls `setViewportRows`, so it
is inert — it keeps its own `termRows − 4 ≥ 6` viewport.

The outer `slice(0, layout.progressLines)` (`:432`) is **retained unchanged**
as the redundant count regression guard (ruling Q4's single-source discipline;
`computeProgressLayout` remains the sole logical grantor and is untouched).

### 2.3 Head semantics per kind — `unitLines` element 0, no new copy

The one-row line is element 0 of `unitLines(u, i, width, isFocused)` for
whatever kind the effective unit is. No new copy, no per-kind floor renderer,
no parallel surface (R-COPY):

| Unit kind | One-row line (element 0) |
|---|---|
| `toolCall` (paired or not) | `→ <label>  <primary-arg>` (`warning`), `+ muted " ✗"` when the paired result `isError === true`; truncated (`:708`) |
| unpaired `toolResult` | `⎿ <label> · <N>b` (`muted`), `+ muted " ✗"` when `isError`; truncated after §2.4 |
| `user` | `accent`+`bold` `user` |
| `assistant` | `success`+`bold` `assistant` |
| `thinking` | `dim` `thinking` (only reachable when `t` makes it visible, `:677-684`) |

The marker prefix is `isFocused`-gated as today (`:697`, `:709`). No
per-kind behavior changes beyond the §2.4 clamp.

### 2.4 Width clamp at the single-head site (ruling Q4)

One clamp, at the single source, `unitLines`' `single`-kind head
(`navigator.ts:723`):

```ts
const out = [truncateToWidth(marker + head, width)];
```

idempotent with the toolCall head's existing clamp at `:708`. The rule — *a
head line that overflows the granted viewport is wrong at any size* — applies
at both viewport sizes; the multi-row change for narrow `single` heads is real
and accepted (Skeptic O6 records it as row 1 of a multi-row render too),
gated by `test/ev34-tool-unit.test.ts` and
`test/ev35-transcript-interaction.test.ts`. Marker-before-truncate ordering is
preserved so a narrow width cannot eat the `▌`. No companion marker change is
needed (Skeptic O7 `closed-green`: the `single`-kind marker is already
`isFocused`-gated).

### 2.5 Never empty

At grant 1 over an empty transcript the projection returns the existing dim
placeholder (`(waiting for output · idle)` when a tail exists, `(no transcript)`
otherwise) — exactly one line, no marker, no header. The projection never
returns `[]`. Green today (`:801`); retained so the new branch cannot regress
it.

### 2.6 Out of scope (explicit)

- `computeProgressLayout` (`extensions/focus-nav.ts:103-121`) — untouched;
  all three generators agreed and O12 reproduces the pinned vectors.
- The header line and its `(on)` suffix — EV-35's subject; at grant 1 the
  header is sliced out, which is the honest state (FLLWUP-37 stays `Backlog`;
  its bullet-3 one-row carve-out is already mandated by this card's
  Acceptance bullet 2, but its overflow case is not consumed).
- Header width clamp (FLLWUP-38) and the legacy `blockLines` renderer
  (FLLWUP-36).
- The full-screen modal path (R-MODAL).
- No new theme tokens, no literal colors/ANSI (AGENTS.md 9.6;
  `test/theme-compliance.test.ts` is a live gate).
- No `follow`-mode badge, no `current/total` indicator, no header pin at one
  row (designer round-1/2 non-goals, unchanged).

## 4. Tests (failing first)

- **T1 (content, red today — the card's own red state).** Multi-line trailing
  assistant/user body, grant 1: the single line contains the effective unit's
  composed-head tokens and **not** the body continuation string. Red at
  `:814-815`.
- **T2 (subject discriminator, R3).** Three tool units, fresh view, `follow`
  on, grant 1: the single line is unit 3's head and `count("▌") === 0`; after
  `G` the line is unit 3's head with `count("▌") === 1`; after `↑` from `G`
  it is unit 2's head with `▌`. Red today; also discriminates R1/R2.
- **T3 (Q5 non-violation).** After any sequence of `poll()` + `render()`
  (new units arriving, `t` toggled) with no key pressed, `focused` is
  unchanged.
- **T4 (frozen grant, red today).** Open progress at termRows 9 with one job
  (grant 2), land a second manifest, `refresh()`, render: the transcript line
  is the effective head, and `activeTranscriptView.viewportRows` equals
  `computeProgressLayout(9, 2).progressLines === 1`; then raise the grant
  (termRows 40 with the same tree content →
  `computeProgressLayout(40, 2).progressLines === 31`) and assert
  `activeTranscriptView.viewportRows === 31` and that a transcript longer
  than the grant emits exactly 31 lines. Red today (`:438-440`, `:628`).
- **T5 (count guard, green today — retained).** For the pinned pairs
  `(7,5)`, `(8,5)`, `(9,11)`, `(40,11)`: `computeProgressLayout` unchanged,
  rendered transcript lines `≥ 1` and `≤ progressLines`, widget total `≤
  treeLines + sepLines + progressLines`.
- **T6 (never empty).** Empty file at grant 1 → exactly 1 line containing
  `(waiting for output · idle)`; missing file → `(no transcript)`.
- **T7 (width).** Grant 1, width 40, unpaired `toolResult` with a 60-char
  label: every returned line satisfies `visibleWidth(line) ≤ 40`. Red today
  (`:723`).
- **T8 (byte parity per kind under the projection — carries Skeptic O8).**
  For each of `user`, `assistant`, `thinking`, unpaired `toolResult`, paired
  `toolCall`: the grant-1 line is byte-equal to the head line the multi-row
  renderer emits for the same unit at a large grant (located by its expected
  literal, cf. `test/ev34-tool-unit.test.ts:47-60`).
- **T9 (parity under projection + modal inertness — carries Skeptic O9).**
  Inline transcript lines are byte-identical to a fresh standalone
  `TranscriptView` given the same JSONL, width and current grant, including at
  grant 1 and across the `(9,2)` transition; a modal-shaped view at its own
  grant (`termRows − 4`) emits the unchanged window.
- **T10 (no regressions).** `test/ev7-council-tree-widget.test.ts`,
