# EV-11 — Bounded decomposition session (design)

Settled design for card EV-11 (epic EPIC-3). This document is the write-up
of the Council's deliberation (rounds 1–3 across owner/principal/designer,
Skeptic step-4 attack, consolidator synthesis with an empty open-judgment
bucket) **as amended by the step-6 product-owner ruling on status-line
placement, which is binding and quoted verbatim under "Binding rulings" and
"Insertion point 3" below**. It derives nothing new; it records what the
deliberation settled. An owner reading only this file must not be able to
reach two different designs — in particular, where exactly the status line
goes, and that the post-Wave-3 seam block does not contain it.

## Goal (from the card)

> The features-new decomposition session ends within a fixed round cap at an
> explicit convergence or fallback outcome on every run, mirroring the
> bounded deliberation of the council command, instead of exchanging for an
> unbounded number of rounds

  rounds, the same exchange cap /council uses** (council.md step 3) — one
  consistent bound across the council's loops.
- **FALLBACK-1 (non-convergence)** — when the cap is hit without
  convergence, the **facilitator drafts the decomposition anyway** and
  carries every open disagreement into the step-3 draft pass, **labeled as
  unresolved**, for the human to settle at the approval gate the command
  already has. No new gate is created.
- **STEP-6 (status-line placement, product-owner, verbatim)**

  > The session status line physically sits in the Part 2 ledger-description
  > paragraph (currently "**Attribution and the disagreement ledger** live
  > at the step-3 gate presentation…"), adjacent to the existing
  > "presented, never written" guard sentence, as a new sentence in its own
  > right. It does **not** live in a new post-Wave-3 seam block before
  > "**Aggregation.**". The post-Wave-3 seam block carries the convergence
  > criterion and the fallback authorship/contents clause; the status line
  > is described alongside the ledger content it annotates.
  >
  > Final copy, inserted adjacent to the existing guard:
  >
  > `Session status: Non-converged after 3 rounds — this is a fallback

  > approval gate. Ledger only — presented, never written.`

  Options rejected by the ruling: (1) the status line in the post-Wave-3

## The rewrite

The settled bound text keeps the two-insertion structure (intro sentence +
post-Wave-3 block). The status line is a presentation element of the same
rewrite, not a bound-structure insertion: per STEP-6 it lands in the Part 2
ledger-description paragraph (Insertion point 3), not in the post-Wave-3
block.

### Insertion point 1 — the intro no-exchange sentence (step 2 intro, before Wave 1)

The step-2 intro currently ends with the sentence "in three waves. You
route, wait, aggregate verbatim, and author nothing." A new sentence lands
immediately after that, before the `**Wave 1 — ...` heading. It must state,
in one breath:

- the session is bounded at **three waves = three rounds**, the same numeric
  cap `/council`'s step 3 exchange uses;
- **no seat is re-dispatched to respond to another seat's position** — the
  three waves are the three rounds; no wave is re-run; no fourth dispatch;
- a **stall re-dispatch is a dispatch retry, not a new round** (the
  carve-out must ride in the same sentence so the cap sentence cannot
  contradict the dispatch discipline's cancel + one re-dispatch rule);
- disagreements are ruled in wave 3, never exchanged.

Drafting latitude (owner's call, per the deliberation): the no-re-dispatch
clause may be the explicit form "no seat is re-dispatched to respond to
another seat's position" (preferred — forecloses a wider error class) or the
shorthand "no re-dispatch". Either satisfies the siting pin, but the clause
must carry the stall-retry carve-out in the same sentence and must be
sited before "Wave 1" in the whitespace-flattened text
(`flat.indexOf(no-re-dispatch clause) < flat.indexOf("Wave 1")`).

### Insertion point 2 — the post-Wave-3 bound block (between Wave 3 and Aggregation)

A `**Bounded session and fallback.**` block lands after the Wave 3
paragraph and before the `**Aggregation.**` paragraph.

**This block carries exactly two components — the convergence criterion and
the fallback outcome. It does NOT carry the session status line, and it
contains no restatement of "presented, never written" in any form.** Per
STEP-6, the status line lives in the Part 2 ledger-description paragraph
(Insertion point 3), and a guard restatement here would be the unauthorized
duplication the ruling rejected (a second guard instance ~1200 chars away
from the existing one creates a new leak surface).

**Convergence criterion.**
- Convergence is recorded at the **fixed endpoint** (after Wave 3, where
  product-owner has ruled last, unconditionally) — never by stopping early.
  council.md's "stop early if positions have stabilised" is **not** imported
  (wave 3 cannot be skipped).
- The session **has converged** when wave 3 has ruled every open-judgment
  dispute the attackers surfaced: **zero open in-scope judgments remain
  after wave 3**, where *open* = unruled by product-owner **AND** not
  settled by a runnable check.
- A **named, ruled dissent is not non-convergence** — a seat that did not
  endorse a card, where product-owner ruled, is a converged run.
- An **escalated, unruled item is non-converged** by construction, and it is
  the fallback's canonical content: product-owner's `<escalation>` fires
  precisely when it may not rule, so the item is open at wave-3 end; it
  rides to the step-3 gate as an unresolved disagreement for the human. The
  criterion sentence must **not** contain the phrase "or explicit
  escalation" under any paraphrase (dogwatch against a superseded round-1
  wording).

**Fallback outcome (FALLBACK-1).**
- When the cap is hit without convergence, the facilitator **drafts the
  decomposition anyway**: the fallback draft is the **mechanical verbatim
  aggregation of all recorded contributions** — identical to the converged
  case, including wave 3's amendments. Never facilitator-authored synthesis,
  never a "most-advanced seat" position as base, never a second authorship
  pass for product-owner (aggregation *is* the draft).
- Every open disagreement is carried into the step-3 draft pass, **labeled
  as unresolved**, for the human to settle at the existing approval gate. No
  new gate, no `Needs Human` stop.
- The dispatch discipline's **double-fail stop is an incomplete-run outcome,
  not the fallback** — the fallback is the aggregation and needs a complete
  wave-3 contribution record.

### Insertion point 3 — the session status line (Part 2 ledger-description paragraph, adjacent to the existing guard)

Per STEP-6, the session status line physically sits in the **Part 2
ledger-description paragraph** of step 2 — the "**Attribution and the
disagreement ledger** live at the step-3 gate presentation…" paragraph,
which ends with the existing **guard sentence** "The ledger is **presented,
never written** — it does not survive onto the on-disk card." The line is
inserted **adjacent to that guard sentence, in its own sentence(s), within
the same paragraph** (directly after the guard sentence, as the paragraph's
closing text). It does not live in the post-Wave-3 seam block (Insertion
point 2), and it is not split across loci (ruling's rejected option 2).

The exact status line, verbatim from the ruling's final copy:

> Session status: Non-converged after 3 rounds — this is a fallback draft.
> The unresolved items below are for your decision at the existing approval
> gate. Ledger only — presented, never written.

Constraints, all binding:

- The line must **not** contain the phrase "every disagreement" (ruled
  dissents keep their rulings and their per-row `Decision: unresolved —
  your call` marker; the row-level marker is identical in both states, so
  the session-level "Non-converged" + "fallback draft" signifiers, and the
  open-items reading of "The unresolved items below", are the only
  distinguishing content).
- The line is **presented, never written**: a Part 2 ledger line, never
  Part 1 card text, never in any card's `Intent` or frontmatter, never
  persisted. Its trailing clause "Ledger only — presented, never written."
  is part of the ruling's final copy and states exactly that property; it
  does not displace or alter the existing guard sentence, which stays
  unchanged in the same paragraph.
- **Co-location (Skeptic step-4 objection 10, resolved by STEP-6):** the
  line sits in the Part 2 ledger-description paragraph, adjacent to the
  existing guard — the pin below checks **adjacency** (the status line
  within ~200 characters of "presented, never written" in the flattened
  text), not mere file-wide co-occurrence. From this locus the adjacency
  is structurally satisfied (~tens of characters from the guard, which sits
  at flat index ~6400 in the current file); from the seam-block locus it is
  not (~1200 chars away), which is why the ruling rejected that placement.

## Test plan

Prose pins on the whitespace-flattened `features-new.md` text, mirroring
`test/prose.test.ts` conventions. All are red until EV-11 lands; none can be
made green by a "any-dissent ⇒ fallback", "or explicit escalation", or
persisted-status implementation:

1. **Cap-parity / pre-dispatch no-exchange siting** — the intro sentence
   states the bound as three waves = three rounds (same numeric cap as
   council.md step 3);
   `flat.indexOf("no seat is re-dispatched to respond to another seat's position") < flat.indexOf("Wave 1")`,
   and `flat` co-contains "a stall re-dispatch is a retry, not a round"
   within the same sentence.
2. **Escalation ≠ convergence** — `expect(flat).not.toContain("or explicit escalation")`
   (dogwatch), and `flat` must co-occur escalation with non-convergence (an
   escalated/unruled item stated as an open disagreement that becomes the
   fallback's content).
3. **Zero-open, not unanimity** — `flat` contains "a named dissent is not
   non-convergence" (structurally equivalent) — pins the ruled-dissent-is-
   converged property against the fallback-fires-on-any-objection
   regression; the criterion states open = unruled by product-owner AND not
   settled by a runnable check.
4. **Bound siting + step-3 pin** — the post-Wave-3 block sits between
   "Wave 3" and "**Aggregation.**", and the status line sits in the Part 2
   paragraph; both precede step 3:
   `flat.indexOf("Session status: Non-converged after 3 rounds") < flat.indexOf("## 3. Draft-then-confirm")`;
   the `## N.` heading set is unchanged; `STEP3_FIXTURE` stays byte-green;
   attribution-free pins stay green; stack-pin stays green.
5. **Session status line — sited in the Part 2 paragraph, presentation-only,
   adjacent to the guard** (status-line siting pin, reworked to the STEP-6
   placement):
   - **Content** — `flat` contains "Session status: Non-converged after 3
     rounds", "this is a fallback draft", and "Ledger only — presented,
     never written", and does **not** contain "every disagreement".
   - **Siting (part 2 paragraph)** —
     `flat.indexOf("Session status: Non-converged after 3 rounds")` lies
     inside the Part 2 ledger-description paragraph: after
     `flat.indexOf("**Attribution and the disagreement ledger**")` and before
     `flat.indexOf("**Part 1 card drafts must be attribution-free.**")`.
   - **Co-location (adjacency, Objection 10)** —
     `|flat.indexOf("Session status: Non-converged after 3 rounds") − flat.indexOf("presented, never written")|`
     is within ~200 chars — physical adjacency to the existing guard, not
     file-wide co-occurrence.
   - **Seam-block negative** — the region between `**Wave 3 —` and
     `**Aggregation.**` contains neither "Session status" nor "presented,
     never written": the post-Wave-3 block carries no status line and no
     guard restatement.
6. **No-early-stop (carried)** — `flat` states convergence is recorded at
   the fixed endpoint and product-owner always runs last — pins against
   importing council.md's "stop early if stabilised".

Behavioral smoke (per SMOKE-1): a scratch-copy run with a forced
non-convergence seed shows exactly three completed rounds, skeptic +
designer dispatched together in round 2, product-owner last in round 3, and
no post-Wave-3 seat dispatch — the runnable evidence that the session ends
within the round cap. Job ids on record.

## Acceptance mapping

- *The procedure text states a numeric round cap, a convergence criterion,
  and a fallback outcome for non-convergence* — the rewrite's components:
  cap sentence (3 rounds), convergence criterion, fallback outcome
  (FALLBACK-1), and the status line presented in the Part 2
  ledger-description paragraph (STEP-6).
- *The stated bound matches the deliberation bounds of council.md in shape
  (fixed cap, explicit stop) rather than inventing a new scheme* — the cap
  is the same numeric 3 as council.md step 3; the stop is explicit
  (converged or fallback, every run ends after wave 3). Deliberately
  different in mechanism: features-new has no re-dispatch exchange loop to
  terminate, so council.md's "stop early if stabilised" is not imported.
- *python3 council/validate.py stays green; step 3's gate text unchanged* —
  validate.py reads cards/board only; step 3 is byte-pinned and untouched.
