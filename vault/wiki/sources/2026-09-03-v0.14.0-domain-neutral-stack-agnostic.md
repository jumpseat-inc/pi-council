The v0.14.0 release — pi-council stops carrying one product's DNA.

## Refactor 1 — domain-neutral seats (`d3a6f38`)

The nine seats, three procedures, wiki pages, and board cards lose every trace
of the EV-charging/PLN/ev-guide domain they were extracted from (PETA SPKLU
examples, importer normalization anecdotes, maplibre tiles, `/healthz`). Two
structural consequences:

- **AGENTS.md convention #1 inverted.** It used to read "seats are opinionated
  on purpose — the domain prose is the product, do not generalize it." It now
  reads "seats are **domain-neutral by design** — seat bodies are written for
  any repository; product-specific grounding comes only from the
  `<repository_grounding>` block `buildSystemPrompt` appends" (see
  [[repository-grounding]]). The prohibition flipped side: do not
  *reintroduce* a hardcoded domain.
- **A prose regression guard** in `test/prose.test.ts` now fails if the
  removed domain vocabulary reappears in packaged seats/procedures.

The seat *roles* are untouched — the steward's portfolio doctrine, the
product-owner's mechanism × user value pair, gate discipline — only the
product-instance prose went. What remains is deliberately generic.

## Refactor 2 — stack-agnostic (`033f450`)

Bun/TypeScript assumptions replaced with "the repo's own tooling":

- **Scaffold `preflight.sh`** drops the `bun`-on-PATH, `package.json`/
  `bun.lock`, and `bun install` checks entirely, replacing them with a
  commented "Project tooling gate" the consumer extends with their own build
  tool / project-marker / install checks (see [[preflight]]).
- Seats and procedures say "a short test", "the test command", "typecheck" —
  never `bun test`/`bunx tsc`. README's requirements list drops the bun
  prerequisite.
- A second prose regression guard enforces the stack-agnostic wording.

## Also in the release

- `7d5bfa3` **archived 28 design specs and implementation plans** under
  `docs/superpowers/` to stubs pointing at this wiki — the wiki is now the
  canonical record; the plans were drift hazards.
- The wiki's own seat/entity pages were de-domained and stack-agnosticized in
  the same commits (designer, owner, principal, product-owner, skeptic,
  steward, preflight, council-job-tree-inline, non-clobbering-scaffold,
  pi-council-overview, sources/2026-08-23-agents, sources/2026-08-24-bugfix-
  seat-prose, sources/2026-08-26-design-ev8, log).

## Why it matters

The package's claim — "the workflow's opinions are the product" — now means
*procedural* opinions only (gate discipline, board rules, seat roles). Domain
knowledge is the consuming repo's job, supplied per-repo via the grounding
block. This is the completion of the extraction: the council extension that
was pulled out of one product's repo is finally product-agnostic.
