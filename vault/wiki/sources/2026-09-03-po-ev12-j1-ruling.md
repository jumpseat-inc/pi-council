# PO ruling — EV-12 J1 (in scope vs out of scope)

Source: `vault/raw/2026-09-03-po-ev12-j1-ruling.md`. Settles the J1 open-judgment
item from EV-12 (EPIC-3).

## Ruling

**In scope.** The "Prompt Then Get Prompted" prose at `README.md:19–21` is
reconciled inside EV-12, as a framing correction in the new content owner and
designer add under the seat table. The Why? intro is not rewritten; only the
prose that contradicts the shipped `/features-new` behavior is corrected. The
correction restates the procedure's real property — the council prepares a
draft, then the human approves at the unconditional draft-then-confirm gate
(steps 0–3 of `council/procedures/features-new.md`; see
[[three-wave-decomposition]] and [[presented-never-written]]).

## Options rejected

- **File as a follow-up** (principal). The disputed prose is not intro/motivation
  — it is the prefatory description of the very procedure the card's goal names,
  and by reading order it conditions every claim below it. Grouping it with
  [[procedure-commands]]'s terse row and [[council-loop]]'s `/council`-only
  stop-early sentence collapses three different surfaces.
- **Rewrite the Why section in full.** Out of scope — it would change the
  author's framing claim; correctness bleeds past the goal.

## Grounding

- The card goal names two properties (bounded multi-seat deliberation +
  unconditional draft-then-confirm); lines 19–21 assert a contradictory third
  ("it'll prompt you for more details").
- Skeptic O2 (`closed-red`): `grep -c prompt council/procedures/features-new.md`
  = 0 — the only human touch-point is the single step-3 approval gate.
- Standing rulings SEATS-1, SMOKE-1, CAP-1, FALLBACK-1 unchanged and binding;
  CAP-1 + FALLBACK-1 + the O1 qualifier define the unconditional gate whose
  absence from lines 19–21 is the defect.

## Reversibility

Cheap — one paragraph replacement at `README.md:19–21` inside the new content
scope; no engine, card, wiki, or AGENTS.md change.
