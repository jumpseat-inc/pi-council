# Product-owner ruling — EV-40 open-judgment (Q1–Q6)

Source: `vault/raw/2026-09-17-po-ev40-ruling.md`. Ruled at step 6 for EV-40
(EPIC-9); the card goal and R5 are immutable.

## Rulings

- **Q1 — surface:** R5's "input bar" is binding. The countdown renders via a
  `CustomEditor` subclass installed with `setEditorComponent`, composing over
  `getEditorComponent()` (single editor slot), rendering as an extra
  editor-region line, intercepting Esc, and forwarding every other key
  (EV-8 forward-unhandled). The designer's status-row alternative is **not
  adopted** (it would reverse R5 — escalation territory) and is filed for the
  designer's own escalation.
- **Q2 — exhaustion:** "Press Enter to try again" means what it says. Enter
  re-sends the original prompt and resets the attempt counter to 1. The errored
  assistant message persists in the JSONL transcript but is filtered from the
  next provider request via a structural, one-pass `context` hook.
- **Q3 — headless abort:** SIGINT to the parent process; runtime teardown
  disarms the timer, no new surface.
- **Q4 — input bar during backoff:** interactive. Typed text accumulates; Esc
  aborts; Enter on a non-empty buffer submits the typed text and aborts the
  retry; Enter on empty is a no-op. The user's prompt wins.
- **Q5 — headless exhaustion:** process exits **75 (`EX_TEMPFAIL`)**; the
  terminal copy is the final stdout line; a wrapper re-invokes. `-p` has no
  stay-alive-and-read-stdin mode (see [[headless-pi]]).
- **Q6 — testability:** partly testable; **T-H1** (SIGINT during headless
  backoff disarms the timer) rides into the step-7 spec as a gate.

## Gates and constraints

- **Must settle before implementation:** P1 (headless timer-deferred send —
  EV-43 only proved send-inside-handler) and D1 (the "malformed" payload
  premise is inferred, not verified). Both need a fake-provider harness.
- **Carried:** O3 literal hygiene (with-colon literal in every test), O2 (pi
  honors `maxDelayMs` at the provider loop; the agent-turn `_prepareRetry`
  applies no cap/jitter), O4 structural filter, O6 surface bans (`setEditorText`
  forbidden; no `placeholder`), O5 (`ctx.isIdle()` cannot distinguish "settled"
  from "nothing happening"), and the `attempt` field-name agreement (EV-39/EV-40
  each mint their own; only the name is shared).
