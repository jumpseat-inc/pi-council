# 2026-09-23 FLLWUP Epic Grouping

The **second phase** of backlog management, following the curation pass
([[2026-09-23-fllwup-backlog-cleanup]]). The 41 remaining open `FLLWUP` cards
were grouped into **7 thematic close-out epics** and re-homed via their `epic:`
fields under commit `8c7913c`. The raw record is
`vault/raw/2026-09-23-fllwup-epic-grouping.md`.

## The two-phase pipeline

Curation reduces the count; grouping gives the residue a delivery home. Neither
is sufficient alone — a curated backlog is still a flat list, and a flat list
cannot be handed to `/features-deliver` as one coherent scope.

**Grouping repairs the `epic: null` orphans curation creates.** Curation nulls
the `epic:` field on every cross-epic merge ([[follow-up-backlog-curation]]);
grouping re-homes all 41 cards, including the 8 orphans. The two operations are
complementary: curation trades *origin attribution* for consolidation, grouping
restores a *home* — but not the origin.

**Origin attribution becomes prose-only.** A child's `epic:` now points at its
new thematic epic; the "filed by EPIC-N" lineage survives only in the new epic's
`Intent` (each of EPIC-16…22 names its source lineage).

## A new epic flavor — the close-out epic

EPIC-1…15 are feature/capability epics whose `goal` is a single falsifiable
sentence. EPIC-16…22 are **close-out epics**: their `goal` is an aggregate
rollup of the children's outcomes ("the filed residuals are all delivered — X,
Y, Z … with the gates green"). This is a distinct epic flavor generated by
backlog close-out, and it sits in tension with the board's "one falsifiable
sentence" goal rule ([[engineering-board]]). Recorded as a boundary, not a
contradiction: the epic's closure is judged on the union of its children.

## Mechanics

- **Ids allocated at fetched HEAD** per [[card-id-allocation]] → EPIC-16…22.
- All 7 epics are `Backlog`; no children promoted yet. Promotion follows
  [[chain-promotion]].
- Grouping is **by subsystem** (`council/gate/**` + `extensions/gate*.ts` for
  EPIC-16, the usage store for EPIC-17, etc.) so each epic is a runnable scope
  rather than a mixed bag.
- `FLLWUP-95` (the pending release bump) was deleted rather than grouped;
  releases are handled manually.
