The designer's first-pass position on EV-9 (Enter-on-a-row opens the
selected subagent's live progress), grounded on the EV-7 widget and the
EV-8 `routeEditorFocus` kernel. Generator position feeding the deliberation;
the binding tiny-regime floor is the [[2026-08-26-po-ev9-tiny-regime-floor]].

## Core position

- **Progress is an inline expansion, not a separate surface.** The same
  below-editor widget renders a `Container`: existing tree rows unchanged,
  plus (only when `surface === "progress"`) a dim divider row
  (`▾ <seat> progress · esc back`) and the existing `TranscriptView.render(width)`
  lines. No `withModalFrame`, no backdrop — the message area above stays
  visible (the Phase-1 inline ruling and the desire for contrast both forbid
  dimming).
- **Shared-VStack hard bound:** the factory's total emitted lines must stay
  ≤ `availableRows = max(1, termRows − 5)`; the tree is the spatial anchor and
  keeps its EV-7 cap, progress gets the remainder.
- **Routing table:** in `surface === "progress"`, Up/Down/`e`/`t`/`f`/`g`/`G`
  forward to `view.handleInput` (TranscriptView's own block nav, follow and
  expand); **Enter is a consumed no-op** (a forcing function against the
  "press Enter again" slip); Escape exits to `"tree"` and disposes the view;
  everything else forwards to `super.handleInput` (forward-unhandled,
  inherited from EV-8 Ruling 2).
- **Freeze the tree's selection while progress is open** — the tree rows do
  not re-sort or re-navigate; the highlighted row stays on the seat whose
  progress is open (other seats' glyphs still tick live).

## Falsifiable predictions

Rendering (pure-seam): the stacked output is `treeRows + divider + viewLines`
by construction; no backdrop/modal chrome; token-only; height budget
`tree+divider+view ≤ remaining`; factory dispose clears timers, reopen
constructs a fresh view. Comprehension (human-settled): which seat am I
watching (highlighted row above), what can I do (divider names esc back +
TranscriptView keymap), how do I leave (Esc).

## Preferences / escalation

Divider `▾ <seat> progress · esc back`; mode label `-- PROGRESS --`; tree-wins
windowing at small budgets. Escalated to PO: the surface-type expansion
(`editor | tree | progress`) and the small-terminal height budget split.
