> ⚠️ **Planned — EPIC-10 is `Backlog` as of 2026-09-20; not shipped.**

Two related gaps EPIC-10 addresses:

1. **Fewer follow-up cards** — runs emit many near-duplicate or
   technically-close `FLLWUP-` cards; the epic merges them rather than filing
   each separately (see [[engineering-board]] step-13 follow-up cadence and
   [[card-id-allocation]]).
2. **Unattended wiki ingest** — every `/council` completion and every
   `/features-deliver` run autonomously ingests its run and learnings into the
   vault wiki ([[llm-wiki]]), escalating judgment calls to the ruling seat
   ([[product-owner]]/[[steward]]) rather than offering or silently dropping the
   step.
