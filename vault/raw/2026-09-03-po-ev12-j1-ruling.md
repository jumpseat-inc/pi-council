# PO ruling — EV-12 J1 (in scope vs out of scope)

## Ruling

**In scope.** The "Prompt Then Get Prompted" prose at `README.md:19–21` is
reconciled within EV-12, as a framing correction in the new content owner and
designer propose to add immediately under the seat table in "What you get."
The Why? intro framing is not rewritten; only the prose that contradicts the
shipped `/features-new` behavior is corrected. Principal's "group with
`vault/wiki/procedure-commands.md` terse row + `council-loop.md` stop-early
framing as one follow-up class" is rejected — those three are not alike.

The framing correction replaces lines 19–21's misdescription with the
procedure's actual property: the council prepares a draft, then the human
approves (the unconditional draft-then-confirm gate, steps 0–3 of
`council/procedures/features-new.md`). No Why/motivation rewrite; the
`How do I get prompted instead of prompting?` block-quote above the
subsection stays as-is. The fix is bounded to the prose that, by the
README's reading order, primes the reader for the procedure, the wiki,
the board, and the council sections below it.

## Options rejected

- **Out of scope, file as a follow-up** (principal). Loses because the
  disputed prose is not motivation/intro in the card's sense — it is the
  prefatory description of the very procedure the card's goal text names
  ("describe features-new decomposition… consistent with the shipped
  procedure text"), it physically sits ahead of the seat table in reading
  order, and it conditions every claim below it (the wiki, board, and
  council subsections the settled D1 read updates are downstream of it).
  The grouping with `[[procedure-commands]]`'s terse row and
  `[[council-loop]]`'s stop-early framing collapses three different
  surfaces: a terse pointer row in the wiki, a `/council`-correct
  stop-early sentence under CAP-1 that does *not* govern `/features-new`,
  and a factually false prefatory claim about a procedure this card is
  already updating.
- **Rewrite the Why section in full** (no seat proposed, but a
  conceivable alternative). Out of scope for EV-12 — the goal's "describe
  features-new decomposition" is forward-looking on the procedure surface,
  and a Why rewrite would change the author's framing claim
  (`How do I get prompted instead of prompting?`). Reversibility is
  cheap; correctness is not — it bleeds out of the goal.

## Grounding

- **Card goal (EV-12.md):** *"the package README and AGENTS.md describe
  features-new decomposition as a bounded multi-seat council deliberation
  that preserves the human draft-then-confirm gate, consistent with the
  shipped procedure text."* The goal names two properties — bounded
  multi-seat deliberation and an unconditional draft-then-confirm gate.
  Lines 19–21 currently assert a third, contradictory property: that "as
  the council deliberates it'll prompt you for more details." A README
  that does not describe the draft-then-confirm flow while its intro
  claims a prompt-flow is, on the goal text, not done.
- **`README.md:7–34`** (the actual file, read directly): the four `###`
  sub-sections under `## Why?` — `Prompt Then Get Prompted` (line 19),
  `LLM Wiki` (23), `Board & Cards` (27), `The Council` (31) — sit *before*
  `## What you get` (line 91), the seat table (line 100+), the Commands
  row (line 185), and the `/features-new` Commands cell that D1 already
  commits to sharpens. By reading order the disputed prose is the
  reader's first claim about procedure behavior — not motivation.
- **Skeptic O2 (closed-red, settled D5):** `grep -c prompt
  council/procedures/features-new.md` = 0; the only human touch-point in
  `/features-new` is the single step-3 approval gate presented in one
  pass. The prose is factually false for the procedure; the contradiction
  is not a soft reading-error, it is the procedure-shape itself rejecting
  the claim.
- **Settled D1 (design converged owner+designer+principal rounds 1–2):**
  the new content is "one paragraph immediately under the seat table in
  *What you get*." The framing correction enters that locus — alongside
  the two load-bearing properties D1 already teaches — without expanding
  scope into a Why section rewrite.
- **Standing rulings SEATS-1, SMOKE-1, CAP-1, FALLBACK-1** are unchanged
  and binding. CAP-1 (three waves, the same cap `/council` uses) confirms
  the bounded multi-seat deliberation the goal names; FALLBACK-1
  (present a draft anyway, carry disagreement as unresolved) plus the
  O1 qualifier the owner adopts at step 7 ("after the full three-wave
  session, you always get a draft to approve") together define the
  unconditional gate whose absence from lines 19–21 is what the framing
  correction restates.
- **Reversibility rationale (rule for the cheapest-to-reverse option
  since neither `vault/wiki/` nor the board history speaks to README-as-
  documentation-surface scope):** a paragraph replacement at lines 19–21
  inside the new content the D1 spec will produce is one local edit,
  pinned by the same reading-order reasoning that motivated it. It
  costs less to reverse than any other option (subsection introduction,
  Why rewrite, follow-up card) because it lives in the same paragraph
  group as the new, convergent content; it cannot drift apart from the
  spec it corrects.

## Reversibility

Cheap. One paragraph replacement at `README.md:19–21` inside the new
content scope. No engine change, no card file change, no wiki change, no
AGENTS.md change. The follow-up option principal proposes is equally
reversible — cost is comparable. The deciding distinction is that the
goal text on a plain reading does not let the dispute's resolution sit
outside the card.
